## Not in this layer

Hull timber, ballast, anchors and ground tackle, rig, provisions and water, the soldiers' personal arms, and cargo (Juliana's siege-carriage wheels). These are most of the mass. They need a hull model built from the measured dimensions below, not from the toneladas.

The people are not counted as mass. The muster lists 976 soldiers and seamen on the three ships in May; Cuéllar says more than a thousand drowned, and the Lord Deputy reported 1,100 bodies counted along the strand (Martin p. 111). The site is a war grave.

The formula fits Lavia and Anunciada exactly and Visón within 2.4%; Regazona (+20%) and San Nicolás (+9%) do not fit (Martin p. 412 suspects 'more fundamental errors' there). Regazona's stated 1079 machos is one end of the v0.7 §28.2 interpolation for Juliana's dimensions, so that estimate inherits the misfit. The toneladas are the machos plus one fifth everywhere except Visón, whose 666 Martin emends to 566. Juliana is not in this document, so her dimensions stay inferred (reconstruction v0.7 §28.2).
