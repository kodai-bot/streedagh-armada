# Superseded

Kept as the record of how the work got here. **Do not quote these numbers.**

- `v1_tonnage_model/`: the first quantitative model. It went from historical tonnage through volume
  to displacement, used Visón at 666 tons (v0.7 emends this to 566) and a length/beam ratio of
  3.3–3.8. v0.7 §2 explains why it is superseded: it "moved too quickly from historical tonnage to
  modern mass", which produced false precision. `streedagh_HEADLINE.md` summarises
  its outputs. `streedagh_guns.py` prints "75 documented guns − 13 recovered ≈ 62 still on or
  under the sites", which assumes every documented gun went down with the ships. That
  assumption is not supported.
- `drafts/`: the v1 site assessment (entity-first; replaced by the signal-field v2) and the
  WordPress-block draft of the "What Streedagh still holds" article (the clean version is in
  `1_signal_field/`).
