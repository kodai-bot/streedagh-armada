## A prediction to test when positions are found (HYPOTHESIS)

If the ships grounded on an outer bar in the breaker line, heeled shoreward and broke up fast, the castles would fall inshore. Guns mounted high should then lie inshore of the keel line, and heavy low guns near the keel and ballast. Stated as an ordering: distance inshore of the keel line increases with `height_rank`. Salvage, later storms (the 2015 exposure) and incomplete recovery all blur this, so it is a tendency to test, not a rule.
