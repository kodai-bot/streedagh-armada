# Streedagh Cargo Manifest & Surviving-Mass Upper Bound
*Bottom-up Monte Carlo (200,000 draws), calibrated to Birch & McElvogue 1999 finds
and the Trinidad Valencera siege-train template. Cross-checked against the hull-model payload ceiling.*

## Anchoring evidence (Birch & McElvogue 1999, IJNA 28.3)
- All three ships = Levant Squadron under Bertendona (La Regazona). **La Lavia = almiranta** (vice-flagship).
- Home ports: Venice, Barcelona, Ragusa (three Mediterranean shipbuilding traditions).
- **Santa María de Visón carried the fleet's medical supplies** (transferred from the
  condemned hospital ship Casa de Paz Grande at Laredo) → de facto field-hospital store.
- **De Cuéllar was aboard La Lavia** (transferred under the Judge Advocate); his eyewitness
  account describes all three breaking up within an hour, <300 of >1000 surviving.
- **Siege train confirmed physically:** 1.5 m spoked carriage wheels (5 on Juliana site),
  matching the 10 disassembled siege-carriage wheels from La Trinidad Valencera (Martin 1979).
- Finds incl.: bronze guns (3 lifted early + 1 under rudder, Juliana), bronze esmeril w/
  arms of Philip II + loaded breech (Visón), two gun-carriages w/ bronze guns incl. a
  truck carriage + a trailed carriage w/ medio sacre, falcon pedrero (Lavia); iron & stone
  shot, lead sheeting, anchors, articulated hull. Bronze guns ID'd vs AGS armament inventories.
- Reported recovered to date: **13 cannon + a gun carriage** (floor for the model).

## Operational template: Terceira / Azores 1583 (the dress rehearsal)
- Santa Cruz: 15,372 men, 98 ships incl. **31 big merchantmen converted as transports**,
  landing craft, galleons, 12 galleys, 2 galleasses. Mission: land an army WITH its
  equipment and supplies on a beach-head and sustain it.
- Defenders (de Chaste): up to 31 stone forts + 13 fascine outposts, trenches, 293 guns →
  shows the battering/entrenching train the attackers had to carry.
- **Same architect:** Santa Cruz wrote from Terceira (9 Aug 1583) urging England next, and
  drafted the original Armada plan. Doctrine, gear and personnel carry straight over to 1588.
- French/English garrison troops permitted to retire → plausible route for the French
  veteran de Cuéllar later met in Sligo (de Cuéllar-at-Terceira per his own letter: to footnote).

## Consistency check
As-loaded durable+organic per ship sits just under the independent hull-model payload
ceiling (Juliana ~402 t vs cap 450-579 t), confirming the manifest fits the physical envelope.

## Top-of-band justification
De Cuéllar (expert master mariner) repeatedly judged these among the largest ships he knew →
licenses reading the UPPER tail of the parametric envelope, not the median.
