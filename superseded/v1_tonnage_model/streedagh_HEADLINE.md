## Ordnance still present
- 75 documented armament guns − 13 recovered = **~62 guns still on/under the sites**, PLUS any siege train.
- Light tier (swivels, minions) ~17 t — wins by COUNT, sits high, recovered first.
- Heavy tier (sacres, culverins) ~27 t + siege train ~13 t — wins by MASS, sits low, **still buried**.

## Why it survived (the key anomaly)
Bronze guns are normally the FIRST thing salvaged (gunmetal > the ship's value). Here they lay
unsalvaged on the surface for 400 years because:
1. The wrecking (1588) opened O'Sullivan Beare's "Fifteen Years War" — NW Ireland was a contested
   warzone 1588–1603 (1589 Connacht rising; Maguire burning Sligo 1593; no army hospital west of
   the Shannon). No secure access for organised salvage.
2. Dynamic Streedagh sand buried the sites quickly.
3. Birch & McElvogue: "No documentary evidence has been found to indicate salvage in the past."
Warzone + rapid burial = an unsalvaged, effectively sealed Armada invasion cargo.
